📜 Rules & Mechanics

  • Team Building: You must select exactly 3 Pokémon to form your battle team.
  • Turn-Based Combat: You and your opponent take turns using elemental attacks or healing moves.
  • Type Advantages: Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, Water beats Fire! Type multipliers deal extra damage.
  • Trainer Cheers: Every 3rd turn, your trainer cheers you on, granting +3 bonus damage!
  • Victory Condition: Defeat all 3 opposing Pokémon to win the championship!
